## ⚑ Active project: Community Value Initiative for AI Infrastructure (`initiative/`)

The `initiative/` directory holds the canonical fundraising/positioning materials for Matt's UVA initiative on data centers and community value. **Before drafting anything related to this initiative — briefs, decks, emails, op-eds, bios — read these files first and treat them as the source of truth:**

1. `initiative/decisions.md` — locked decisions (anchor number, structure, roster, positioning, messaging architecture) and open items. Do not re-litigate locked decisions.
2. `initiative/master-case.md` — the canonical prose master case. All derivative cuts pull language from here.
3. `initiative/founder-positioning.md` — verified founder evidence bank + four audience-calibrated bio variants. **Never lead with "physician" alone or emergency medicine; sentence two of any bio is always the market-transformation receipt (GHP → LEED/GRESB), never medicine.**
4. `initiative/cut-1-brian-brief.md` — the one-page internal cut; the model for future audience cuts.

Key invariants: aphorism/thesis = **"Build the relationship, not just the project"**; universal hook = "The next bottleneck in AI isn't compute — it's the relationship with the communities that host it"; frame = the one first negotiation (no second first negotiation; permit is the wrong prize); method line (never the headline) = making community value **measurable and investable**; arms = equip the negotiation / sustain the relationship / scale the practice; anchor stat = Data Center Watch Q1 2026 (~$130B blocked/delayed); proof structure = owned GHP precedent first, IIHS supporting; lifecycle facts are supporting evidence only. Full architecture + stakeholder map: `initiative/messaging-map.md`. Bracketed [placeholders] stay visibly bracketed until Matt verifies them.
