# Messaging Map — Community Value Initiative
*The full narrative architecture, locked 2026-08-05. This is the component sheet for designing artifacts (briefs, decks, leave-behinds, op-eds). All language traceable to `master-case.md`.*

## The spine: problem → aspiration → why us → what → how → who benefits

**1. Problem (reframed — the one negotiation).** Every project has one moment when operator and community sit down with everything open. It happens once — *there is no second first negotiation.* Today it's run as a permitting gate: rushed, adversarial, minimum disclosure. Run that way it yields a permit and a grievance — and increasingly neither (~$130B blocked/delayed in Q1 2026 alone).

**2. Why the gate fails structurally.** The permit is the wrong prize. Shells are engineered for 25+ years; hardware refreshes every 3–5 years with a new architecture every 18 months (8–10 hardware generations per building; the industry can't even agree how long a GPU lasts). The project *will* change in ways no one can specify at signing. Only a relationship covers that — and communities aren't wrong to say no when they have no instrument to negotiate with.

**3. Aspiration (the aphorism).** **Build the relationship, not just the project.** A durable, trust-based working relationship between the AI infrastructure industry and its host communities, founded in a deliberate, transparent first negotiation, producing maximum shared value through decades of change. Business necessity, not PR: social license, financial materiality at $10B+/campus, near-certain regulation, changing assets.

**4. Why us (the equipment gap).** The negotiation fails for lack of equipment, not goodwill: no shared measure of value, no trusted referee, no process discipline, nothing to return to when the project changes. Building that equipment is a university's job — and this founder has run the exact playbook (GHP → LEED/GRESB).

**5. What (three arms = one sentence).** **Equip the negotiation** (practitioner tools, private until validated) · **Sustain the relationship** (dataset, papers, benchmark — the shared ledger) · **Scale the practice** (standards partnerships: GRESB, LEED, EGC).

**6. How (five workstreams).** Industry engagement & learning → convening → research → tool development → standards development. All under published principles: transparency, rigor, independence, service to both sides of the table.

## Headline system

- **Universal hook (headline):** *The next bottleneck in AI isn't compute. It's the relationship with the communities that host it.*
- **Aphorism (the thesis in one line):** *Build the relationship, not just the project.*
  - Industry variant: *Develop the relationship, not just the site.*
  - Founder-voice variant (convenings, teaching, op-ed): *Design the relationship, not just the project.*
  - Prose variant: *Negotiate the relationship, not the permit.*
- **Urgency line:** *There is no second first negotiation.*
- **Capital-audience line:** *The relationship is the longest-lived asset in the project* — it outlasts the hardware, the workloads, and arguably the building.
- **Method line (always subordinate, never the headline):** making community value *measurable and investable*.

## Stakeholder map — who benefits, and the message register for each

| Stakeholder | What they get | Register / lead message |
|---|---|---|
| Host communities & residents | A credible seat and a real ledger — costs and benefits priced, not asserted; leverage without moratorium | "You aren't wrong. You've been under-equipped." |
| Community advocates / activists | Independent rigorous data replacing anecdote; a constructive channel that doesn't require "no" | Evidence as power |
| AI infrastructure industry (operators, developers, hyperscalers) | Faster, more durable approvals; social license that survives change; de-risked capital | D-line (cheapest insurance / durable license); *Develop the relationship, not just the site* |
| Local & state officials, regulators | Neutral evidence base for conditions and agreements; templates instead of one-off fights | The referee they can cite |
| Economic development & U.S. competitiveness actors (local → federal) | The build-out proceeds without steamrolling communities | Both/and: competitiveness *through* community value |
| Capital & broader real estate industry | Community risk becomes priceable and underwritable; portfolio benchmark (GRESB heritage) | The asset line |
| Standards bodies (GRESB, LEED, EGC) | Validated content for the next material topic | Benchmark before certify |
| Research community | New dataset, new field | The Lenox collaboration model |

**Terminology note:** umbrella term for the industry side is **"the AI infrastructure industry"** (covers operators, developers, and the hyperscalers behind them); use "operators" when speaking of NDA engagement specifically.

## Usage rules

- The aphorism or the hook opens every artifact; the method line never leads.
- Lifecycle facts (25+yr shell / 3–5yr hardware / 18-month architectures / depreciation fight) are *supporting evidence for uncertainty*, one beat, never the headline stack.
- Anchor stat stays: Data Center Watch Q1 2026, ~$130B, 75+ projects.
- Every stakeholder message must be derivable from the spine — no audience gets a message that contradicts another audience's.
- Principles language stays light: one sentence (transparency, rigor, independence, both sides of the table), not a manifesto.
