## The frame (locked)

**Identity line:** Physician, public health researcher, and Associate Professor at the University of Virginia School of Medicine, with a global reputation for translational research on how built environments shape public health, well-being, and social value.

**Career one-liner (his own words, HIP bio):** His work makes health promotion in buildings, communities, and cities *measurable and investable*.

**The positioning argument:** The initiative is not a pivot — it is the second application of a market-transformation playbook the founder has already run at global scale: take a diffuse, unpriced value (then: health; now: community value), build rigorous instruments for it, validate them with practitioners, and install them inside the standards and benchmarks real-estate capital already obeys.

**Do not lead with:** emergency medicine (historical ties), bare "physician," or clinical framing. Clinical training (pediatrics + preventive medicine, MD/MPH Emory) is trust-layer detail, not headline.

## The ten-year receipts (Green Health Partnership)

GHP = applied research initiative between UVA School of Medicine and the U.S. Green Building Council, funded by the Robert Wood Johnson Foundation, with Matt as Principal Investigator. Verified outcomes:

- **LEED pilot credit.** GHP led development of the *Integrative Process for Health Promotion*, launched as a pilot credit in LEED v4 BD+C — explicitly modeled on health impact assessment. Variants launched in the 2015 Enterprise Green Communities Criteria (Enterprise Community Partners) and Green Star (Green Building Council of Australia). **Used by 200+ built environment projects in the U.S. and Australia** (per GHP Phase II impact report). Seven-project LEED Health Process case-study series published with USGBC.
- **GRESB Health & Well-Being Module (2016).** The first portfolio-level health assessment for real estate practitioners and investors — used by **252 real estate portfolios** and their investors to evaluate management of **32,000+ real estate assets** (Phase II impact report); GRESB's benchmark serves institutional investors managing trillions in assets.
- **Professional capacity.** The American Institute of Architects released continuing-education modules based specifically on GHP's Health Process Credit, reaching its membership of **90,000+ registered U.S. architects**.
- **Field-building.** Founding member of the *Joint Call to Action to Promote Healthy Communities* linking the American Public Health Association with USGBC and other national built-environment organizations.
- **Enterprise Green Communities.** GHP developed resident health & well-being criteria; Matt now serves on the **Technical Working Group for the 2026 EGC Criteria**.

Post-GHP: **Chief Medical Officer, International WELL Building Institute (2021–2023)** — led integration of health science into the WELL Building Standard and WELL Ratings, adopted across tens of thousands of projects. **ARPA-H advisor** (BREATHE program, indoor air quality). **Founder & Principal, Health Intent Partners LLC** — including current health impact assessment work with data center developers inside live projects.

## The continuity quote (use everywhere)

From Matt's Greenbuild 2015 session, "Making Healthy Property Investable" — the initiative's thesis, stated a decade early:

> "Within the real estate industry, the health impacts of transportation, community development, and other construction projects, both positive and negative, continue to operate largely as economic externalities: unmeasured, unregulated, and for the most part unconsidered. This lack of transparency limits communities' ability to effectively advocate for real estate investment that best promotes their health and well-being. It also limits market incentives for innovation… Going forward, healthy places must also become investable."

Replace "health" with "community value" and "construction projects" with "AI infrastructure" and this is the master case's Section 1. The argument, the method, and the messenger have a ten-year public record.

## GHP theory-of-change language worth borrowing

- "Using green building as a model… applying tools of **market transformation**."
- "Establishing health promotion as a **shared focus and capacity within the real estate industry**."
- Externalities framing: "unmeasured, unregulated, and for the most part unconsidered."
