The teaching program and its sprint format. Ten years of accumulated material — syllabi, workshop plans, handouts, executive briefs for deans and partners.

The opportunity here is different from the other domains: this work is already done, repeatedly, and mostly undocumented as reusable assets. The design system is captured in `mdp-hds-brand`; what is not captured is which materials are worth templating.

A plausible paper sits inside this program — ten years and 200+ students is a real n — but that has never been scoped.
