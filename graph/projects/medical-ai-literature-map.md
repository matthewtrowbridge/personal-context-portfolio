Cross-disciplinary literature retrieval — mapping how medical AI research connects across fields that do not cite each other. Purely academic, which makes it the cleanest candidate to move onto UVA-provisioned accounts.

Blocked on a question rather than on work: what UVA actually provides, and at what data classification. Until that is answered the corpus should not scale, because moving it later is more expensive than starting it in the right place.

This is the project that most benefits from batch processing, so the answer on research-computing access changes its shape materially.
