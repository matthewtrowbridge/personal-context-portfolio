Matt's role supporting research within the Emergency Medicine residency: helping residents get projects scoped, through IRB, and to presentation or publication.

Structurally distinct from the HIP work in one way that matters: resident projects can involve human subjects. Anything carrying `phi` or `irb` data classes stays out of this repo entirely and lives in a UVA-approved location. What lives here is the program shape, the people, and the pipeline — never the data.

`uva-irb-navigator` handles the submission mechanics.
